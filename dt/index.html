The decisions

    
From insight to action.

    
One method for every scale: source real data, score it with a method written down, put it in 3D so anyone can click a building and see why, and say plainly what it cannot tell you.

    

      

        
01 · Veiligheidsregio Utrecht · Jun–Sep 2026

        
Which neighbourhood needs help first in a heatwave?

        
A heatwave lands on people who are old, alone, or under a roof with no shade. The Heat Resilience Digital Twin scores all 111 Utrecht buurten on 28 weighted indicators, ranks 11 candidate wijken, then drops to street level: every building at its real height, classified by Landsat heat measured around it.

02 · Space & Water Nexus Hackathon · 1–3 Oct 2026

        
Where do we look first when a discharge is suspected?

        
Rijnland samples at few places, weekly to monthly at best, so nobody can say where something started. The Langeraar Water Quality Digital Twin ranks buildings and structures that are plausible places to start looking and compares the stations that have a real lab record. A screening tool, not proof of a source.

How it works

    
From open data to a handover in five steps.

    
    
      
  1. 01
    Source
    CBS, RIVM, KNMI, Landsat, BAG, 3D BAG and agency registers, each with its origin recorded.
    2. 
      
  2. 02
    Model
    A written scoring method per decision. Weights, directions and cut-offs are explicit.
    3. 
      
  3. 03
    Twin
    Put it in 3D so a non-specialist can click a building and see why it scores as it does.
    4. 
      
  4. 04
    Decide
    Rank where to act first, with limits stated next to every number.
    5. 
      
  5. 05
    Hand over
    Method report, guide, walkthrough film and a session with the team who will use it.
    6. 
    

    
↻ Learn & improve: new data shows what to refine next


    
Built from data. Defensible by design.

    
    
      
  • 01
    Every figure is real
    Nothing is sample data. Each layer carries its source, date and what it compares.
    • 
      
  • 02
    Screening, not proof
    Scores are signals from public data. Grey means “not evaluated”, never “safe”.
    • 
      
  • 03
    Missing shown as missing
    Where CBS or RIVM suppress a value, the map says so instead of drawing a zero.
    • 
      
  • 04
    Surface is not air
    Land surface temperature is labelled as such and never mixed with air temperature.
    • 
      
  • 05
    Open standards
    BAG, CBS, PDOK, KNMI and GeoPackage, so the work travels between tools and teams.
    • 
      
  • 06
    Handed over, not left behind
    The VRU twin went to the resilience team and the OKT-P programme with documentation.
